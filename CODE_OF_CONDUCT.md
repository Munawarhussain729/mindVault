# Code of Conduct

MindVault is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to learn and
contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and keep it specific and kind.
- Welcome newcomers. Questions are fine, and so are first pull requests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who keep doing so. To report a problem,
use the repository's private
[security advisory form](https://github.com/Munawarhussain729/mindVault/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
